Solara Heat is an original top-down open-world crime game that runs entirely in the browser. You are Nico Reyes, a getaway driver in the coastal city of Port Solara (3200×2400), working off a $5,000 debt to fixer Marisol Vega through six story missions: a timed package run, stealing a Vantara GT sports car, a pickup that turns into a three-star escape, a six-checkpoint street race, and a finale to seize a ledger and flee to the marina under a five-star wanted level. The city is alive with traffic and 34 pedestrians; police chase, ram and shoot; you can fight back on foot with a pistol, watch your bust meter, and follow it all on the HUD and minimap. Progress saves in the browser, and the sound is synthesized — no assets, no build step, no network.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open the local address it prints.
